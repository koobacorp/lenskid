LENSKID v1.0 - install on Mac (DaVinci Resolve 18/19/20, free or Studio)

You get two things in this zip:
  LENSKID.ofx.bundle  - the looks plugin (grain, glow, wobble, damage + colour)
  LUTs/LENSKID        - 7 colour-only LUTs (.cube), one per look

THE PLUGIN
1. Quit DaVinci Resolve.
2. Open Terminal and paste these 3 lines one by one (Enter after each; it asks for your
   Mac password, typing shows nothing - that's normal):

   sudo mkdir -p /Library/OFX/Plugins
   sudo rm -rf /Library/OFX/Plugins/LENSKID.ofx.bundle
   sudo cp -R ~/Downloads/LENSKID.ofx.bundle /Library/OFX/Plugins/

   (If you unzipped somewhere else, drag LENSKID.ofx.bundle onto the Terminal window
   instead of typing ~/Downloads/LENSKID.ofx.bundle.)
3. Open Resolve. Effects > OpenFX > Filters > LENSKID. Drop it on a clip (Edit page) or
   on a node (Color page), then pick a Look in the Inspector.

THE LUTS
1. Copy the "LENSKID" folder into:
   /Library/Application Support/Blackmagic Design/DaVinci Resolve/LUT/
   (Terminal:  sudo cp -R ~/Downloads/LUTs/LENSKID "/Library/Application Support/Blackmagic Design/DaVinci Resolve/LUT/" )
2. Restart Resolve (or Project Settings > Color Management > Update Lists).
3. Color page: open the LUTs panel (top left) > LENSKID, and drag a look onto a node.
   Or right-click a node > LUT > LENSKID > pick one.

HOW THE CONTROLS WORK
- Look: the 7 looks. Every slider's 50 = that look's normal amount (0 = off, 100 = double).
- Amount %: the whole look. 0 = the clip untouched.
- Grain, Glow / Halation, Wobble / Lens, Distortion / Damage: see README.md for what each
  slider does in each look.
- Real colour blend %: pulls the colours back toward the real ones but keeps the texture.
  On Thermal Heart, 30-50% keeps faces readable.
- More options (closed by default): Vignette, 18 fps feel (Super 8), Light leaks, Date stamp
  and its text (Dashcam), Beat hits + BPM + Beat offset (Melt), Random seed.

TIPS
- Footage should be in Rec.709 (normal phone / camera footage, or after your colour-space
  conversion). On LOG footage, put LENSKID or the LUT after your conversion node.
- LENSKID runs on the CPU. On 4K timelines, playback can stutter; renders are fine.
  Use Playback > Render Cache > Smart, or proxies, while editing.
